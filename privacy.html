Vesper (package studio.vesper.player) · last updated 25 September 2026

Privacy policy

This is not legal advice. Vesper is a media player. It does not provide, host, sell, or recommend television channels, playlists, or VOD.

Who we are

Vesper Digital Studio publishes the Android TV app Vesper, application id studio.vesper.player. Contact vesper_player@proton.me.

What stays on your TV

  • Playlist URLs, Xtream usernames, and related settings.
  • The portal password, in the Android Keystore-backed vault when the device allows it.
  • M3U stream URLs exactly as you paste them (they may include provider tokens).
  • Parental PIN as a salted hash (a household lock, not strong cryptography).
  • Watch history, favorites, EPG cache, and optional recordings you choose to keep.
  • Optional OpenSubtitles username and API keys you type in Settings.

We do not run an account system. We do not receive your playlists.

What leaves the device

Network traffic goes to:

  • Hosts you configure — your IPTV / Xtream / EPG / stream servers, including credentials those URLs contain.
  • Optional metadata services you can leave unused: TMDB, Apple iTunes Search, Wikipedia, TVMaze, OMDb (if you add a key), OpenSubtitles, and YouTube (trailers in an official IFrame or the YouTube app). Those lookups send titles, TMDB ids, and your IP address to those operators under their policies.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat — when subscription support is enabled, Google Play processes purchases and RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, product and purchase identifiers, and subscription status on our behalf to validate Premium access and understand purchase lifecycle events.

Purchase validation is required to provide paid features and is not processed only ephemerally. It is used for app functionality and subscription analytics and is encrypted in transit. Vesper does not receive or store your payment-card details. Google Play and RevenueCat process data under Google's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

There is no third-party crash reporter in v1.

Subscriptions

Vesper may offer optional monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions through Google Play. Any trial eligibility, trial duration, recurring price, billing interval, and renewal terms are shown by Google Play before purchase. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play.

Recordings and backup

Optional recordings and file backups use storage you pick (app folder or a SAF folder). In-app backup export omits passwords by default. Android Auto Backup of the library is disabled.

Children

Vesper is a general-audience player. The PIN is a household lock, not a children’s service.

Deletion

Uninstall the app, or delete a playlist in Settings, to remove that library from the device. Recordings in a folder you picked stay until you delete them. To request deletion of Vesper's RevenueCat customer record, email vesper_player@proton.me. We may ask for a purchase or anonymous app user identifier so we can locate the correct record. Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google under its own retention obligations. Deleting app data or uninstalling Vesper does not cancel an active subscription.

Changes

We will date this page when the policy changes. Keep using the app only if you accept the current version.
