How to sideload
  1. TV: Open Downloader or a browser app on your TV, enter the TV APK direct link, and install.
  2. Phone: Download the Phone APK directly on your device and tap to install.
  3. Optionally verify the SHA-256 value with SHA256SUMS.txt.
  4. Install, open Vesper, and add your authorised source.

FAQ • Legal

A player. Not a content service.

Does Vesper include channels?

No. First launch asks you to add your own authorised source.

Which edition should I download?

Download the Android TV Edition for smart TVs, Google TV, Fire TV, soundbars, and streaming boxes (built for D-pad remote navigation and direct USB storage access). Download the Phone & Tablet Edition for Android phones and tablets (built for touch gestures and mobile player controls).

Where is my information stored?

Playlist configuration, settings, favourites, and watch history remain on your Android TV or mobile device unless a feature must contact your provider or an optional metadata service.

How are downloads verified?

Every public APK is checked for package, version, production signature, and SHA-256 checksum before publication.
