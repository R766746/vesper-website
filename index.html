How to sideload
  1. Allow installs from your trusted Downloader or file-manager app.
  2. Open this site on the TV and download the production APK.
  3. Optionally compare its SHA-256 value with the published checksum.
  4. Install, open Vesper, and add your authorised source.
Updates: Install the new APK over the old version. Your data stays when the package and signing certificate match.
ADB:
adb install -r app-release.apk
FAQ • Legal

A player. Not a content service.

Does Vesper include channels?

No. First launch asks you to add your own authorised source.

Where is my information stored?

Playlist configuration, settings, favourites, and watch history remain on your Android TV unless a feature must contact your provider or an optional metadata service.

How are downloads verified?

Every public APK is checked for package, version, production signature, and SHA-256 checksum before publication.
